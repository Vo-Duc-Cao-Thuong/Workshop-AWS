### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment was very friendly, open, and professional. Everyone in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program was always willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties, even outside working hours. The workspace was clean, comfortable, and conducive to maintaining focus. However, I believe the program could further improve by organizing more informal gatherings or regular team-building activities to strengthen collaboration and build closer relationships among team members.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
My mentor provided dedicated guidance, explained technical concepts clearly, and consistently encouraged me to ask questions whenever I encountered challenges. The team administrators were also highly supportive, assisting with administrative procedures, documentation, and ensuring that I had everything needed to work effectively. I especially appreciated that my mentor encouraged independent thinking, experimentation, and problem-solving instead of simply providing ready-made solutions.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The tasks assigned during the internship were highly relevant to my academic background while also exposing me to practical technologies and real-world scenarios that I had not previously experienced at university. This allowed me to strengthen my theoretical foundation while developing a more practical approach to solving real business and technical problems.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Throughout the internship, I significantly improved many important skills, including project management, teamwork, communication, and professional working practices in a corporate environment. The knowledge-sharing sessions conducted by mentors were particularly valuable, providing practical insights and helping me gain a clearer vision of my future career path.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The organizational culture was positive, respectful, and collaborative. Team members treated each other with mutual respect while maintaining a professional yet comfortable working atmosphere. Whenever the project faced tight deadlines, everyone willingly supported one another regardless of their roles or responsibilities. This created a strong sense of belonging and teamwork throughout the internship.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The flexible working schedule made it easy for me to balance my university studies with internship responsibilities. In addition, having the opportunity to participate in internal training sessions and technical knowledge-sharing activities greatly contributed to my personal and professional development.

### Responses to Additional Questions

- What did you find **most satisfying** during your internship?
  What I appreciated the most was the supportive and open-minded culture of the entire team. Every question I raised was answered thoroughly, which motivated me to keep learning, improving, and becoming more confident throughout the internship.
- What do you think the company **should improve** for future interns?
  It would be beneficial to provide more detailed orientation sessions during the first few weeks, especially regarding technical standards, development workflows, and project collaboration processes. This would help new interns adapt more quickly and become productive sooner.
- If recommending to a friend, would you **suggest they intern here**? Why or why not?
  Absolutely. It is an excellent environment for gaining hands-on experience with real-world projects, learning from experienced mentors, and developing both technical expertise and essential soft skills.

  I would recommend organizing more technical sharing sessions or seminars where mentors and team members can discuss emerging technologies, industry trends, and best practices.
- Would you like to continue this program in the future?
  I would be delighted to have the opportunity to continue contributing to and growing with the First Cloud AI Journey program or the team in future projects.
- Any other comments (free sharing):
  I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the First Cloud AI Journey organizing committee, the mentors, and the administrative team for creating such an outstanding internship experience. This program has provided me with invaluable knowledge, practical experience, and a solid foundation for my future career. I wish FCAJ continued success and hope it will continue inspiring and supporting many future generations of talented interns.
